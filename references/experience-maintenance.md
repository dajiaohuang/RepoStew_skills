# Experience maintenance

Periodically select completed, verified and reusable methods. State applicability,
exceptions and repository/issue/commit evidence. Deduplicate against references.
Raw cases remain with repos; private findings never become public lessons.
Update a relevant phase reference or a focused linked reference; recurring reliable
operations become tested scripts. No universal rule per incident.
Keep SKILL.md/shared prefixes short and stable; publish coherent batches, not
per-leaf context changes. No extra knowledge service or automatic schedule implied.
